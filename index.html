The Mystery Collection

A WOMAN
IN CABIN 10

One mysterious disappearance. An empty cabin. A ship full of secrets. What happens when nobody believes the truth you witnessed?

AUTHOR: RUTH WARE YEAR: 2016 GENRE: PSYCHOLOGICAL THRILLER
01 / Book Introduction

A Journey Into Fear and Suspense

What if you witnessed something terrible, but nobody believed you? What if the evidence disappeared and everyone around you insisted that you were wrong? A Woman in Cabin 10, written by British author Ruth Ware, is a gripping psychological thriller that takes readers into a world of secrets, fear, deception and unexpected danger. Set aboard a luxurious cruise ship travelling through the cold Norwegian waters, the novel keeps readers questioning what is real and who can be trusted.

02 / The Story

What Happens on Cabin 10?

A Dream Assignment Turns Into a Nightmare

Laura "Lo" Blacklock is a travel journalist who receives an exciting opportunity to travel aboard the luxurious cruise ship Aurora.

But her dream assignment takes a terrifying turn when she witnesses someone falling overboard from the neighbouring Cabin 10 during a stormy night.

When she reports the incident, nobody believes her. The passenger list shows that Cabin 10 was empty. There is no missing passenger and no evidence of anyone having stayed there.

Determined to discover the truth, Lo begins her own investigation. As she searches for answers, strange events, hidden secrets and dangerous threats begin to surround her.

With nowhere to escape in the middle of the sea, Lo must discover whom she can trust before it is too late.

03 / Major Themes

What Makes This Book Interesting?

01

Psychological Suspense

The story makes readers question reality, memory and the truth behind every incident.

02

Trust and Betrayal

People who appear trustworthy may hide secrets. The story explores the difficulty of knowing whom to believe. .

03

Fear and Survival

Lo faces frightening situations and must find the courage to protect herself.

04

Truth and Deception

The novel explores how lies, manipulation and hidden identities can influence people.

04 / Beyond the Pages

Lessons We Can Connect to Real Life

✦

1. Believe in Yourself

Sometimes people may doubt your words. Do not lose confidence. Think clearly and look for facts.

✦

2. Mental Health Matters

Stress, anxiety and emotional struggles should never be reasons to disrespect or dismiss someone.

✦

3. Think Before You Trust

Not everyone who smiles at you has good intentions. Build trust through actions, honesty and consistency.

✦

4. Courage Is Important

Facing difficult situations requires courage. Speaking up when something is wrong can make a difference.

✦

5. Never Stop Asking Questions

Curiosity helps us learn. Asking the right questions and examining evidence can help us understand the truth.

05 / Think About It

Questions for Every Reader

01. Have you ever experienced a situation where nobody believed your side of the story?

02. How would you react if everyone denied something you had personally witnessed?

03. Is it always easy to distinguish between truth and deception?

04. What is more important during difficult situations: courage, patience or logical thinking?

"Sometimes, the greatest mystery is not what happened, but why nobody believes you."
